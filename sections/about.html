About me

I am an incoming Ph.D. student in Computer Science at the University of Maryland, College Park, advised by Prof. Alan (Zaoxing) Liu. My research lies at the intersection of cloud infrastructure and machine learning systems. I am particularly interested in two directions: (1) designing low-latency and cost-efficient observability solutions for large-scale cloud environments, and (2) building advanced telemetry for ML, with a current focus on monitoring, debugging, and analyzing the performance of LLM serving systems.

Prior to my Ph.D. studies, I received my B.S. in Computer Science from Dian Nuswantoro University in 2026.
